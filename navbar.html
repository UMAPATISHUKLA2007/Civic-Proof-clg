HOW IT WORKS

✦

Easy steps to report civic issues, track their progress and make your community a better place.

01
📝

Report

Click on "Report a Complaint" and start submitting the civic issue you noticed.

02
📷

Upload & Describe

Upload a clear photo, automatically capture your location and describe the problem.

03
✦

AI Verification

Our AI checks the submitted report and verifies whether the issue appears genuine.

04
👥

Public Visibility

Verified issues become visible to the community so everyone can track their current status.

05
📋

Action by Authority

The concerned authority receives the report and takes the necessary action to resolve it.

06
✓

Resolved

Once the issue is fixed, the authority uploads an "After" photo as proof of resolution and transparency.
