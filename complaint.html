Visual & Layout Principles

  • Dual-Column Workflow: Separates input controls from dynamic output results to maintain clear focus.
  • Clear Color Hierarchy: Red/Amber/Green indicators instantly communicate authenticity risk levels to the public without ambiguity.
  • Visual Evidence First: Spatial overlays and frame-by-frame heatmaps build public trust by showing "where" manipulation occurred.
  • Low Cognitive Load: Direct summaries for citizens, with expandable granular details for researchers and journalists.

Essential UI Features

  • Multi-Input Flexibility: Accepts direct text claims, social URLs, audio clips, and high-res video files seamlessly.
  • Explainable Scorecard: Replaces black-box percentages with segmented breakdown metrics (Visual, Audio, Context).
  • Human-in-the-Loop Escalation: "Dispute / Request Human Review" button allows flagging edge-case errors to expert moderators.
  • Auditability: One-click exportable PDF reports featuring tamper-proof cryptographic hashes for legal/news use.
